# Living in the Light of Revelation

## Scripture Reference and Study List

### New Heavens and New Earth

#### **Old Testament**

- Isaiah 65:17–25 — God creates new heavens and a new earth; former things not remembered
- Isaiah 66:22–23 — the new heavens and new earth will endure before the Lord

#### **New Testament**

- Matthew 19:28 — "the regeneration" (palingenesia), Jesus on the throne of his glory
- Acts 3:19–21 — "the restoration of all things"
- Romans 8:19–23 — creation's groaning, awaiting liberation from decay
- 2 Peter 3:10–13 — the day of the Lord, dissolution of the heavens, new heavens and new earth where righteousness dwells
- Revelation 21:1–8 — the new heaven and new earth, no more sea, God dwelling with man
- Revelation 21:9–22:5 — the New Jerusalem, the river and tree of life

### Christ's Second Coming (Parousia)

#### **Old Testament**

- Daniel 7:13–14 — one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven, given dominion
- Psalm 50:3–4 — God comes and does not keep silence
- Isaiah 63:1–6 — the warrior coming from Edom, garments stained
- Joel 2:1–2, 30–31 — the day of the Lord, sun turned to darkness
- Zechariah 12:10 — they will look on him whom they have pierced
- Zechariah 14:4–5 — his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives
- Malachi 3:1–2; 4:1–3 — the messenger of the covenant, the day burning like an oven

##### **Gospels**

- Matthew 24:3, 27, 29–31, 36–44 — the Olivet Discourse, sign of the Son of Man
- Matthew 25:31–46 — the Son of Man comes in glory, sheep and goats
- Mark 13:24–27
- Luke 21:25–28
- John 14:1–3 — "I will come again and will take you to myself"

##### **Epistles**

- Acts 1:9–11 — "this Jesus... will come in the same way"
- 1 Corinthians 15:20–28, 51–58 — resurrection order, the last trumpet
- 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18 — the Lord's descent, the dead in Christ rise
- 1 Thessalonians 5:1–11 — the day of the Lord like a thief
- 2 Thessalonians 1:5–10 — Jesus revealed from heaven with his mighty angels
- 2 Thessalonians 2:1–12 — the man of lawlessness and the coming (parousia)
- Titus 2:11–13 — "our blessed hope, the appearing of the glory"
- Hebrews 9:28 — Christ will appear a second time to save
- James 5:7–8 — the coming of the Lord is at hand
- 1 Peter 1:7, 13; 4:13 — revelation of Jesus Christ
- 2 Peter 3:3–13 — scoffers, the day of the Lord
- 1 John 2:28; 3:2–3 — confidence at his appearing
- Jude 14–15 — the Lord comes with ten thousands of his holy ones

##### **Revelation itself**

- Revelation 1:7 — "Behold, he is coming with the clouds"
- Revelation 19:11–16 — the rider on the white horse, Faithful and True
- Revelation 22:7, 12, 20 — "I am coming soon"

## Other OT Background Woven Throughout Revelation

- **Daniel** 2 (statue/kingdoms), 7 (beasts, Ancient of Days, son of man), 8–9 (visions, seventy weeks), 12 (resurrection, time of the end)
- **Ezekiel** 1; 9–10 (throne-chariot vision, four living creatures), 37 (dry bones, resurrection), 38–39 (Gog and Magog), 40–48 (new temple, river of life)
- **Isaiah** 6 (throne vision, "Holy, holy, holy"), 11 (root of Jesse), 24–27 (Isaiah's "little apocalypse"), 34 (judgment on the nations), 53 (the slain Lamb)
- **Zechariah** 1–6 (night visions, horsemen, lampstands), 4 (two olive trees/witnesses)
- **Exodus** 7–12 (plague pattern behind the trumpets and bowls), 19 (theophany at Sinai), 25–30 (tabernacle imagery)
- **Genesis** 1–3 (creation, tree of life, the serpent)
- **Jeremiah** 50–51 (oracles against Babylon, echoed in Revelation 18)
- **Psalms** 2 (the nations rage, God's anointed king), 96–99 (enthronement psalms, worship scenes)
- **Joel** 2–3 (locusts, day of the Lord, the nations gathered for judgment)
